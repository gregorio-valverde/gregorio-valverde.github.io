I complete one of the most demanding and formative stages of my life. During the degree, I realise that some of my strongest skills are analytical thinking, abstraction and problem solving.

I enjoy Physics, but I also start to understand that I do not want to spend my entire professional life in the field. I particularly enjoy programming, structuring problems, working with data and presenting results. I also become increasingly interested in business and process design.

That is when I start to see Physics not as the end of the road, but as the discipline that gave me the way of thinking I want to bring into my professional career.
