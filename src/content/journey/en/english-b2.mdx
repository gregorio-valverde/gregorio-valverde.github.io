For many years, English had been my weak point and one of those goals I always ended up postponing.

I decide to change the way I approach it and turn it into a routine alongside work. Gradually, I recover knowledge, improve my comprehension and, above all, regain confidence when expressing myself.

I eventually obtain the Cambridge B2 qualification.

For me, this is not really the end of the journey. It is the point when I stop seeing English as a limitation and start seeing it as another skill I can keep developing. The next goal is C1.
