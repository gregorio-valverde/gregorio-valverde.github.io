After an intense first period of professional learning, I have the opportunity to join Grupo Hozono Global as a data specialist.

My perspective changes significantly here. I move from consulting to understanding a company from the inside and taking part directly in its transformation.

One of the main challenges is the lack of a centralised information repository, so it is time to apply everything I have learned. Together with my colleagues, I begin designing a data platform capable of centralising integrations, automations and analytical processes.

During this stage I discover dbt and go much deeper into documentation, testing, modelling and engineering best practices. We also work on properly separating development and production environments and improving how processes are deployed and maintained.

One of the most important lessons, however, is not purely technical. As the platform grows, I also start training and supporting colleagues so they can develop their own automations and data processes.

It is a stage in which I continue growing technically while also developing my ability to explain, teach and guide other people.
